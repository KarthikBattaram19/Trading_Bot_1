1. Background
Volatility is one of the few market variables that can be traded independently of price direction. Professional quantitative trading firms and market makers routinely exploit inefficiencies in volatility through sophisticated strategies such as Statistical Arbitrage, Gamma Scalping, and Vega Trading. These strategies rely on mathematical models, continuous portfolio rebalancing, option pricing theory, and real-time market data to generate consistent risk-adjusted returns.
However, retail traders face significant structural disadvantages when attempting to implement these strategies, including:
•	Limited access to institutional-grade data 
•	Higher transaction costs 
•	Limited computational infrastructure 
•	Lack of automated execution systems 
•	Manual portfolio management 
•	Inability to continuously hedge option positions 
•	Limited access to advanced quantitative research 
Despite these constraints, advances in Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), algorithmic trading platforms, and broker APIs present an opportunity to bridge much of the knowledge and execution gap between retail and institutional participants.
This project aims to design and develop an AI-assisted quantitative volatility trading framework specifically optimized for retail traders. The framework will leverage concepts from Statistical Arbitrage, Volatility Trading, Gamma Scalping, and Vega Scalping while incorporating modern AI techniques for knowledge retrieval, strategy generation, risk management, and trade decision support.

2. Problem Statement
The primary challenge is to develop a systematic, statistically robust, and practically executable volatility trading strategy that enables a retail trader to identify, evaluate, execute, and manage volatility-based trades using institutional-grade concepts without requiring institutional infrastructure.
The framework should integrate:
•	Statistical Arbitrage 
•	Cointegration Analysis 
•	Volatility Forecasting 
•	Gamma Scalping 
•	Vega Scalping 
•	AI-assisted Decision Making 
•	Risk Management 
•	Automated Knowledge Retrieval using RAG 
•	Strategy Optimization 
•	Trade Analytics 
while accounting for practical retail constraints including:
•	Capital limitations 
•	Transaction costs 
•	Bid-ask spread 
•	Slippage 
•	Margin requirements 
•	Limited market data 
•	Manual or semi-automated execution 
•	Position sizing constraints

4. Research Questions
The project seeks to answer the following questions:
1.	Can Statistical Arbitrage be effectively adapted for retail trading despite data and execution limitations? 
2.	Can Gamma Scalping remain profitable after accounting for transaction costs and manual hedging? 
3.	Under what market conditions does Vega Trading provide consistent risk-adjusted returns? 
4.	Can AI-assisted decision support improve retail traders' understanding and execution of volatility strategies? 
5.	How accurately can a RAG-based knowledge system retrieve quantitative trading concepts from domain-specific literature? 
6.	Can an integrated framework combining Statistical Arbitrage, Gamma Scalping, and Vega Trading outperform standalone approaches? 
7.	What is the optimal balance between automation and manual intervention for retail volatility trading?

9. Success Criteria
The framework will be considered successful if it can:
•	Reliably retrieve and explain concepts from the supplied PDFs through the RAG system. 
•	Identify statistically valid trading opportunities using cointegration and volatility metrics. 
•	Generate actionable gamma and vega management recommendations while accounting for retail constraints. 
•	Demonstrate, through backtesting, favorable risk-adjusted performance (e.g., improved Sharpe ratio, controlled drawdowns, and consistent execution after realistic transaction costs). 
•	Deliver transparent, explainable AI-assisted trade recommendations that help a retail trader understand both the rationale and the associated risks.

Overall Vision
The ultimate goal is not to replicate the infrastructure of an institutional market-making desk, but to create an AI-assisted quantitative trading platform tailored to retail traders. By combining the theoretical foundations from the four PDF resources with RAG, LLM reasoning, statistical modeling, and disciplined risk management, the system aims to make sophisticated volatility trading concepts practical, interpretable, and executable within the real-world constraints of a retail trading environment.
